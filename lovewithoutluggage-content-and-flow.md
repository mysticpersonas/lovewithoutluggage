## Things to Fix in the Redesign (inconsistencies found in the current copy)

1. **Program name mismatch:** the page title and OG title say **"Love Before Luggage"**, but the program is **"Love Without Luggage."**
2. **Wrong co-founder name:** the "Why it works" paragraph says "Michelle Fox & **Laura Perna**". Elsewhere the program is led by Dr. Travis & Michelle Fox.
3. **Audience mismatch:** the program is positioned for **couples**, but that same paragraph says "guiding **women like you**", and the meta keywords include "**womans groups**" (also misspelled).
4. **"Lead architect" claim appears twice:** both Travis ("a lead architect") and Michelle ("the lead architect") are credited for the Mind Personas Human Identity Architecture.
5. **Years of experience conflict:** Travis's bio says "over 25 years" and later "more than 30 years."
6. **Brand naming is inconsistent:** the page uses Mind Persona™, Mind Personas™, MIND Persona System, Mystic Personas, Mystic Persona framework and MindPersona®. It does not mention MPHIOS™ or PersonaForce™.
7. **Gendered session language:** "Husband + Travis / Wife + Michelle" assumes heterosexual married couples, while the qualifier list includes relationships that are "romantic or not."
8. **"Level 1 / Level 2" vs "Weeks 1–3 / Weeks 4–6":** these are two different structures and the page never connects them.
9. **Session math is unclear:** the format says 45–60 min sessions, but the first call is described as a "30-minute live group call." It is also unclear whether that kickoff call counts toward the 2 sessions per week.
10. **Typos and formatting:** "Breakfree" should be "Break free". "Copyrights 2025" should be "© 2025". One bullet in Michelle's "leads" list is broken. The orphan "Risk-Free" heading reads like a button with no clear action.
11. **Heading semantics:** most body statements are set as H1s, including the full manifesto block. For SEO there should be one H1, with proper H2/H3 levels below it.
12. **Missing trust elements:** no testimonials, no FAQ, no pricing or investment info, and no explanation of what happens after "Reserve Your Spot."
